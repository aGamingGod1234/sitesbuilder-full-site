## 2. Data we may receive

When someone contacts us or requests a preview, we may receive:

- name, business name and role;
- phone number, email address and contact preferences;
- current website or social link;
- information about the business, services, desired improvements and preferred package;
- logos, copy, images, product/service information and other material supplied for a preview or project;
- messages, approvals, feedback, proposal, invoice and payment-status records;
- technical logs such as IP address, browser/device type, request time, referring page and security events;
- non-sensitive website interaction events and Web Vitals where analytics is enabled.

Do not send unnecessary sensitive personal data through the preview request.
