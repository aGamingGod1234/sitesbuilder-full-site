## 3. Free preview

The free preview is one non-production visual direction for the first two landing-page sections, normally the hero and one following section. It may be supplied as watermarked desktop/mobile stills or a controlled non-production preview.

- No payment is due before preview approval.
- One consolidated adjustment request to the preview direction is included.
- The preview is not a completed website, production code, source file, editable design file, full copywriting service or launch-ready asset.
- Ownership and reuse rights do not transfer with the free preview.
- Local Web Works may decline or defer a request before starting based on fit, capacity, legality or missing inputs.
- A usual target is 2–3 working days after fit, capacity and required inputs are confirmed; the actual date is confirmed in writing.

The requester confirms they are authorised to supply and permit use of all submitted names, logos, images, copy and other material for the preview.

## 4. Project start and payment

Standard website projects use:

- 50% project-start payment after preview approval, written scope, required content/access and commercial details are confirmed.
- 50% final payment within seven calendar days after written staging acceptance, or after included revision rounds are complete and the deliverable materially matches the written scope, and before public launch, repository/file handoff or account transfer.

The project-start payment reserves capacity and becomes non-refundable once production work starts, except where applicable law or the accepted proposal requires otherwise. Third-party purchases require written approval and may be payable in advance.

If the customer disputes material scope conformity in good faith, they must identify the written-scope mismatch within five business days. Local Web Works receives one reasonable cure opportunity and responds with a correction or written scope explanation before treating the disputed final amount as due.

Late payment may pause work, launch, handoff, support or hosting after reasonable written notice, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Delivery timing

The public 3–4 working-day statement applies only to qualifying Essential and Enhanced builds after all start conditions are complete: approved scope, required content, account access and first payment. It refers to staging presentation, not necessarily public launch.

Custom websites, integrations, migrations, customer delays and third-party approvals use the timeline stated in the proposal. Dates move where required inputs, feedback, access or approved third-party services are late.

## 6. Revision rounds

One revision round is one consolidated feedback submission from the named authorised approver against the agreed scope.

A revision round does not include a new concept, page, section, layout, feature, integration, strategy, content campaign or fragmented feedback from multiple stakeholders. Extra work is quoted and approved before it begins.

Feedback is due within five business days of a review request. After ten business days without required feedback or material, the project may pause and be rebooked subject to availability. After thirty calendar days without response, it may be marked dormant; completed work and committed costs may be invoiced. Dormancy is not automatic acceptance.

## 7. Staging, acceptance and cancellation

Local Web Works supplies a staging link or equivalent review artefact when the agreed work is ready for review. Acceptance occurs when the authorised approver confirms approval in writing or when included revision rounds are completed and the delivered work materially matches the written scope.

If the customer cancels after production begins, Local Web Works may retain amounts covering completed work, reserved capacity and approved third-party costs, and may invoice additional completed work not covered by prior payment. Any mandatory legal cancellation/refund right takes precedence.

A fourteen-calendar-day defect warranty covers reproducible faults where delivered functionality does not match the written scope. It excludes new requests, customer/third-party changes, content edits and external-service failures.

## 11. Monthly care

Care plans are month-to-month unless the proposal states otherwise. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 Singapore time, excluding Singapore public holidays. Response targets are acknowledgement/triage targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

A minor change request is one consolidated request affecting existing content, images, links, metadata or styling within the existing layout and requiring no more than approximately thirty minutes. It excludes new pages, sections, layouts, features, integrations, migrations, campaigns and redesigns.

- Essential Care: one request/month; first response within three business days.
- Full Care: two requests/month; first response within two business days.
- SEO Care: up to four requests/month; first response within one business day; monthly agreed analytics/indexation report and bounded technical/on-page improvements; no ranking/traffic/lead/revenue guarantee.
- Complete Care: up to six requests/month; same-business-day acknowledgement before 14:00, otherwise next business day; highest queue priority in business hours; one quarterly thirty-minute review call; no 24/7 emergency SLA.

Unused requests do not roll over. Overage/out-of-scope work requires approval. Cancellation applies before the next renewal; the current paid period is not refunded after work, hosting or reserved capacity begins except where law requires otherwise. If outstanding invoices are paid, the agreed export/handoff is made available before managed hosting ends. Local Web Works targets delivery within seven business days of a valid cancellation request and maintains a reasonable migration overlap through the paid period. Deployment history and customer content may be deleted thirty days after service ends, subject to legal/accounting retention and the Privacy Policy.
